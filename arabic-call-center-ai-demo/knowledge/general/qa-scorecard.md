## Scoring criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Greeted the customer and confirmed the reason for the call | 15% |
| Verified identity/key details before acting | 20% |
| Gave an accurate, complete answer | 30% |
| Tone — polite, clear, and empathetic | 20% |
| Closed the call properly (summary + next steps) | 15% |

## Automatic flags
- Missing required disclosure or identity check → flag for review.
- Long silence (over 15 seconds) → flag for review.
- Negative sentiment with no de-escalation attempt → flag for review.
