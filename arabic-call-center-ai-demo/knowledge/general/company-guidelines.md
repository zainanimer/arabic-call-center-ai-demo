## Tone
- Warm, direct, and respectful. Keep responses concise.
- Always confirm key details (dates, numbers, names) before finalizing a request.

## Escalation
- Safety issues, complaints about staff, or legal questions should be escalated to a supervisor immediately, not answered directly.
- If the customer is upset, acknowledge their frustration before offering a solution.

## Identity verification
- Verify the customer's registered email or phone number before making account changes.
